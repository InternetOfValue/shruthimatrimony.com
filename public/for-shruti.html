A NOTE TO SHRUTI HAASAN & HER TEAM

The dragon is fictional.
The invitation is real.

Before the paperwork, a conversation?

Hi Shruti, and hello to the people looking out for her.

I’m Moses. This website is an independent creative response to a playful podcast moment. The matrimony hook opens a story about doing the work to become a better partner: self-awareness, care, and living your values.

This isn’t an official matchmaking service, and it doesn’t offer access to you. You haven’t endorsed this project or agreed to participate. Completing a challenge earns nobody a claim on your time, attention, or affection.

A little less litigation.
A little more imagination?

My hope is that this lands as a creative expression, with humour and respect. If something here concerns you, I’d welcome a direct conversation and the chance to address it. The dragon has enough fire. We can keep the correspondence civil.

Why a dragon goddess?

Seraphina is a fictional dragon goddess I’m building a larger mythology around. She holds the tension at the heart of this story: ferocity and tenderness, the monster we acknowledge, the divine we practise, and the work of bringing them together.

You inspired that imaginative starting point. Seraphina is her own fictional character; your life and choices remain entirely your own.

My dream casting?
You, as Seraphina.

In my imagination, the role needs someone who can carry both vulnerability and volcanic presence. That’s why I picture you. This is a creative wish, not an announced casting or an attached project.

I’d love to share the lore, why Seraphina came into my life, and the wider world I’m building around her.

If this takes off,
let’s build something together.

I’m open to discussing a revenue share, an equity partnership, or a creative collaboration if there’s mutual interest. This is an invitation to explore possibilities; any arrangement would need a separate agreement we both choose.

No presumed endorsement. No presumed partnership. Just an open door.

Moses Sam Paul Johnraj
Creator · Seraphina / HouseOfBros / The Internet of Value
