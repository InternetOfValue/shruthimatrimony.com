Date Yourself: data use

Signing up registers your interest in an independent programme in development. It does not enrol you in a scheduled cohort or offer a date or introduction.

The creator, Moses Sam Paul Johnraj, uses your name, email, optional phone number, chosen focus and intention to contact you about Date Yourself and organise its proposed trails. Only provide what you are comfortable sharing; the phone number and personal intention are optional.

Submissions are stored in Netlify Forms under the creator’s account. Your contact details are not published on this website or committed to its public GitHub repository. There are no advertising trackers on this site.

You can ask for your entry to be removed or stop further programme contact by replying to a programme message.
