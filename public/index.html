THE STORY / A LITTLE CONTEXT

A few things,
before you begin.

Is this affiliated with Shruti Haasan?+

No. Shruthi Matrimony is an independent creative experiment by Moses Sam Paul Johnraj. Shruti Haasan has not endorsed it, and it is not an official matchmaking service.

What is Date Yourself?+

Date Yourself is a proposed 90-day journey through three 30-day trails: Monster, Divine and Integration. The focus is self-awareness, acting on your values and building consistency.

Does the challenge offer a date with Shruti?+

No. Completing a trail or joining the interest list does not offer a date, an introduction or access to Shruti Haasan. Relationships remain a mutual choice.

What happens when I sign up?+

Signup records your interest in Date Yourself. It does not enrol you in a scheduled cohort. Your contact details, chosen focus and optional intention are used to contact you about the proposed programme.

Can I explore the trails now?+

Yes. The Monster, Divine and Integration links open the existing self-guided trails on HouseOfBros. The Date Yourself form here is a separate interest list for the proposed journey.
